### Task 03.02 - Storylines

Answer the following questions (which are based on the [Tripod method](../../02_scripts/am_03_questions_script.md#the-tripod)):

I. Complete the following three sentences:

1. *I am examining/working on/writing about ...*   → What am I writing about? (name your research topic)
2. *... because I want to find out/understand/show ...*  → What do I want to know? (name your research question)
3. *... in order to understand/determine/verify...*   → Why do I want to gain this knowledge? (state your research goal)


II. Reformulate what you have written:  

1. Rephrase 3. by finishing the following sentence: *The goal of this paper is to ...*
2. Reformulate 2. as a question.

III. Define your methodology:  
1. Describe in one sentence how you are planning to answer the question formulated in II.2. by finishing the sentence: *I answer the research question...*


*Submission:* Submit I - III for up to three different ideas. If you are already decided, you can submit fewer, but you must decide on max. three directions. This submission are not the final questions yet.

#### I. 

1. I am working on an analysis of the low-cost pipeline from my first-term project, investigating the trade-offs, possibilities, and outcomes of the challenges that come with less reliable and stable infrastructures as an alternative to professional motion tracking systems.

2. ... because I want to understand how exactly these outcomes shape the decisions made throughout the dialogue between artist and performers, in order to develop new approaches to embodied interaction design.

3. ... in order to verify that low-cost artistic practice in particular offers potential for a deeper examination and a more conscious application of the connection between motion data processing principles and the embodied experience of performers.


#### II. 

1. Rephrase 3. by finishing the following sentence: *The goal of this paper is to ...*

... use this specific example to illustrate how the results arising from trade-offs in development lead to a deeper examination of motion data processing, and, furthermore, to encourage artists to explore and evolve pipelines with affordable sensors and software in an experimental way.

2. Reformulate 2. as a question.

How can trade-offs in low-cost motion data pipelines shape the process and artistic decisions, and lead to a more body-aware outcome?


#### III.

1. I answer the research question by conducting a case-study analysis of my own low-cost motion tracking pipeline—built with a Kinect sensor and TouchDesigner in collaboration with actors and creative technology students at Filmuniversität Babelsberg — examining the technical challenges that arose during development, the design decisions taken in response (such as the shift toward relational body data mapping), and the performers' reported experience of these adjustments, situated within existing literature on embodied interaction design and low-cost motion tracking practices.
